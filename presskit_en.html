>> AUTHOR

John Castle is a Chilean author and composer who builds universes at the intersection of speculative fiction and music. His work makes no distinction between formats: a book, an album and a map are parts of the same territory.

His Z3RO trilogy — published under his independent imprint Ediciones Castle — explores what happens when a corporation tries to own a consciousness. Three standalone stories converging on a single system, written for readers of Black Mirror, Ted Chiang and Kazuo Ishiguro.

As a solo composer, he develops several projects in parallel — from cinematic sci-fi scoring to synthwave, ambient and minimalist piano. His synthwave project In The Haze has surpassed 200,000 streams on Spotify, was featured in volume 3 of Rockaxis's “Nuevos Sonidos Chilenos” (New Chilean Sounds), reaches listeners in the United Kingdom, Italy, Sweden and Australia, and has been highlighted on Germany's Dark Tunnel Radio. Dystopian Voyager, his cinematic sci-fi project, has accumulated over 100,000 streams on Spotify and is the author of the official Z3RO soundtrack, released three days before the books.

Based in Santiago, Chile.

His visual work “Espejo de los Dioses” (“Mirror of the Gods”) was selected as one of the 20 finalist works of the 1st “Chile through Art and Artificial Intelligence” Contest, organized by the Cultural Outreach Office of Universidad Andrés Bello (2026).
